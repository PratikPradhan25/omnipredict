Honesty

This engine cannot guess the next colour “all the time.” On a fair random game the published call is 50/50 and the expected accuracy is about 50%. Confidence is measured walk-forward accuracy with a Wilson interval and a sample size — not the chance the next colour is right. Ten correct published calls in a row on a fair game is 1 in 1,024 by luck (0.5^10). The master only publishes a colour when the shrunk ensemble’s Wilson 95% lower bound is above 50% on at least 30 hard calls.
